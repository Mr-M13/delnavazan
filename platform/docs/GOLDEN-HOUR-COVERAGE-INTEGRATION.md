# Golden Hour Coverage Atlas — retirement note

The regional demand-window atlas has been retired from the administrator UI. The
unified seven-day instrument coverage clock now carries one aggregated Golden
Hours ring and shows regional demand details on segment selection.

Finance-owned window configuration, pay policy, history and calculations are
unchanged. `AcademyCoverageReadService` and `AcademyCoverageView` remain
read-only historical/finance helpers; the Academy Overview no longer links to or
renders the atlas.

See [INSTRUMENT-COVERAGE-CLOCK.md](INSTRUMENT-COVERAGE-CLOCK.md) for the current
projection and [QUIET-HOURS-ADVISORY.md](QUIET-HOURS-ADVISORY.md) for the
advisory-only Quiet Hours policy.
