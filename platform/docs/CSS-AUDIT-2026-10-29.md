# CSS audit, design-system consolidation and portal presentation (2026-10-29)

- **Baseline:** `origin/main` at `b67f510e1b26686faa90f1ba1268d15882d802fe`.
- **Branch:** `refactor/css-design-system-consolidation`.
- **Scope:** `theme/assets/css/*`, shared enqueue logic, teacher/student template
  presentation, and source-level CSS contracts. No attendance, enrolment,
  payment, scheduling or replacement business rule was changed.

## 1. Method

1. Parsed all seven stylesheets with a structural tokenizer (rules, media
   context, layers, declarations, specificity and `!important` counts).
2. Cross-checked every class selector against PHP templates, patterns,
   JavaScript, WordPress-generated classes and known dynamic state patterns.
3. Rendered the real teacher/student template parts with synthetic display-only
   models and the real header/homepage/booking HTML against the stylesheets.
4. Captured before/after screenshots through Chrome DevTools Protocol at
   320, 375, 390, 430, 768, 1024 and 1440 px.
5. Measured document `scrollWidth` with the legacy global `overflow-x: clip`
   temporarily disabled, so hidden overflow could not mask a regression.
6. Checked form/control names, duplicate IDs, image alternatives, `lang` and
   `dir` on every rendered page.

## 3. Design system

`theme.css` remains the authoritative token owner. New semantic aliases:

- Surfaces: `--dzn-color-surface-muted`, `--dzn-color-surface-sunken`.
- Text/action/border: existing tokens retained; `--dzn-color-border-strong`.
- Status: `--dzn-color-{success,warning,error,information}-surface` and
  matching `-border` tokens.
- Elevation: `--dzn-rgb-{brand,ink,paper,gold,on-dark}` channel tokens for
  translucent shadows and overlays.
- Typography: `--dzn-leading-{body,tight,ui}`.
- Shape/control: `--dzn-radius-{xs,sm,md,lg,pill}`,
  `--dzn-control-height`, `--dzn-control-height-sm`,
  `--dzn-focus-{width,offset}`.
- Layers: `--dzn-z-{appbar,tabs,floating,dialog,header}`.

Existing public colours (turquoise `#1e7b70`/`#104a4d`, pomegranate
`#a33e48`, ivory `#fbf8f2`, saffron `#d4ab4f`) are unchanged. Token contrast
was checked: all body/status/action combinations meet WCAG 2.2 AA for normal
text; primary text is 12.8:1 on the surface token.

Route ownership remains unchanged and correct:

- `theme.css` — global shell, typography, public content, components.
- `portal.css` — Student Portal only.
- `teacher-portal.css` — Teacher Portal only.
- `booking.css` — booking route only.
- `operations.css` — operations route only.
- `editor.css` — block editor only.

No additional stylesheet was added: splitting the already shared, cached
`theme.css` would trade a small portal saving for FOUC risk and duplicate
critical shell rules. All route stylesheets depend on `delnavazan-theme`.

## 4. Teacher portal improvements

- One compact card grid: time/date, student, course, schedule summary, state
  label, actions. Today cards keep a dedicated action column; future cards use
  a compact chevron and calendar link.
- Disclosure arrow works on every card. The general reschedule guidance moved
  once under the classes heading instead of repeating in every form.
- Attention cards now distinguish absence, replacement, reschedule, technical,
  intro, flexible-term and admin states with semantic borders and labels.
- Availability and profile forms have `min-inline-size: 0` at every grid level,
  preventing control intrinsic widths from escaping the card at 320 px.
- Mobile app bar and four-column tab bar use safe-area insets, opaque surfaces
  and 54 px targets.
- Forced-colours, reduced-motion and print guards retained.

## 5. Student portal improvements

- Consolidated the duplicated mobile shell into the single responsive block.
- The upcoming card, schedule rows, reschedule cards and tabs now share the
  same radius, spacing, focus and status language as the teacher portal.
- Long Persian/Latin names wrap; schedule actions stack at 35 rem.
- Disabled, read-only and empty states remain visible; no warning colour is used
  for ordinary pending work.
- Removed duplicate font-size/line-height and shell declarations.

## 8. Known limitations and follow-ups

- Authenticated production Student/Teacher sessions were not available to this
  audit. The portal evidence renders the real template parts and CSS with the
  Theme’s synthetic non-production demo model, not a live account. Authenticated
  smoke remains part of the production release check.
- Several historical homepage selectors (`.dzn-facts`, `.dzn-process`,
  `.dzn-pricing__grid`, `.dzn-home-hero__note`) are absent from current
  patterns and the live homepage, but could still exist in stored block content.
  They were retained pending a content-level audit rather than deleted on
  source evidence alone.
- `theme.css` remains one shared file. A future route-level split should be
  measured against real cached-page performance and FOUC risk.
- Deployment and production smoke results are recorded in the PR/deployment
  record, not inferred from source inspection.
