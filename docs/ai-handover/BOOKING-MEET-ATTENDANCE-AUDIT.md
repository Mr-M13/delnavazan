## Confirmed architecture and integration
1. **Teacher outcome fallback**: `platform/src/Portals/PostIntroTeacherOutcomeController.php:10-37` registers `admin_post_dzn_post_intro_outcome`, checks a WordPress nonce, resolves teacher principal, verifies active teacher authority, validates confirmation UID and delegates to `PostIntroPreparationService::recordTeacherOutcome()`. It redirects back to the teacher portal. The service's precise continuation effects require further trace.
2. **Meet attendance evidence**: `platform/src/Integrations/GoogleWorkspace/MeetAttendanceService.php:15-48` discovers participant sessions; lines 96-146 require a verified account-to-participant mapping and configured provider system actor before canonical evidence intake. Unmapped or failed intake is persisted as deferred. Its header explicitly says it does not itself mark lessons completed. This is deliberate and protects against attendance by display-name alone.
3. **Post-payment activation**: `platform/src/Integrations/PostIntroSettlementHook.php:15-97` enqueues activation alongside verified payment settlement and raises funding target when subsequent instalments settle. `platform/src/Integrations/PostIntroActivationWorker.php:9-64` schedules a two-minute WP-Cron worker with leased claims and eight-attempt limit; lines 101 onward provide operator requeue and execution paths. The activation worker checks effective funded sessions rather than trusting the browser.
4. **Administrative recovery**: `platform/src/Core/Application/PostIntroRecoveryService.php:73-94` invokes settlement hook, requeue and single-job execution; `platform/src/Admin/Controller/PostIntroAdminController.php:336-337` exposes requeue/run actions. Inspect capability, nonce and presentation before recommending changes.
5. **Frontend teacher Meet navigation**: `theme/assets/js/meet-entry.js:12-37` opens a named popup for the teacher's Meet form and polls `window.closed` to redirect to finish screen. `theme/template-parts/classroom/meet.php:222,238` supplies forms and finish URL.

## Priority findings / risks to validate
- **INT-01 (high operational priority, not confirmed defect): Identity-mapping dependency.** Google Meet sessions without verified mapping remain deferred and cannot automatically qualify attendance. Verify teacher/student onboarding creates or guides verified Meet account mapping, and ensure administrator UI surfaces `unmapped`, `ambiguous`, `verified_intake_deferred` and `verified_intake_failed` with safe recovery. Test manual teacher completion as fallback without bypassing canonical attendance rules.
- **INT-02 (medium, UX): Mobile Meet exit handling is browser-window-dependent.** The `meet-entry.js` popup-close flow may not detect an Android/iOS app handoff or a reused external tab. The fallback is a separate tab, but automatic finish-screen navigation is not guaranteed. Add a persistent explicit “Finish class / record outcome” control and verify Android installed-app deep-link behaviour before considering JS changes.
- **INT-03 (high operational priority): Cron scheduling ≠ worker execution.** Activation is queued, leased and retryable, but requires WP-Cron/real cron execution. `deployment/README.md:246,394` explicitly expects the activation and completion hooks to be scheduled; `deployment/qa/production-release-diagnostic.php:391-399` reports cron-disabled state. Verify actual execution age, overdue jobs, failed jobs, and staff-visible recovery, not just hook registration.
- **INT-04 (medium, UX): Teacher outcome handler redirects generic `unavailable` for every exception.** `PostIntroTeacherOutcomeController.php:29-35` discards the specific failure cause. Maintain safe user-facing errors but provide correlated staff diagnostics so teachers can distinguish already-confirmed, stale booking, authority, and transient failures.
- **INT-05 (high acceptance requirement): Confirm end-to-end sequence** intro acceptance → Meet provisioning/co-host/link → verified or manual attendance → post-intro preparation → student payment offer → Stripe verified settlement → funded lesson activation → student/teacher schedule and notification. Include retry, duplicate request, stale UI and failure injection tests.
